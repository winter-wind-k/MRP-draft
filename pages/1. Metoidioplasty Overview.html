Metoidioplasty Overview

What is Metoidioplasty?

Metoidioplasty is a gender-affirming surgery that creates a penis from your existing tissue (clitoris and labia). The word “metoidioplasty” comes from the combination of the Greek word Meta- meaning towards, Oidion- meaning male genitalia, and Plastos- meaning shaping or molding. The primary goal is to create a penis (5-9 cm long on average) with full sensation using the existing genital tissue. Metoidioplasty is also called “meta” for short.
